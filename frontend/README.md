
The app is called Solvi, inspired by the word “resolve.” It is a request management
system, similar to an ITSM (IT Service Management) platform, designed for
managing support and customer service tickets.

Solvi is part of a larger ecosystem of solutions. It works in conjunction with
OrderRequester, a portal that allows customers to submit orders, request quotes, or request services
from a company and track the status of their requests. While
OrderRequester is the customer-facing interface, Solvi centralizes the management of these requests,
allowing teams to organize, track, and resolve them
efficiently.

### Features

- Order management
- Client management
- Product management
